HOT DOG CART

Build it. Sauce it. Toss it. Ring the bell!

About

Run a claymation hot dog cart on a sunny city street. Read each customer's order, build the hot dog with a bun, a sausage and their toppings, then ring the bell and toss it over. Neat hot dogs earn tips; wrong ones cost you a life. How many days can you keep the cart rolling? More features are coming soon.

Support & FAQ

How do I add sauce?

Tap a bottle to pick it up, drag to aim it over the hot dog, then hold still to start pouring. Lift your finger to stop.

What makes a hot dog "messy"?

A crooked sausage or bun, or topping that lands off the sausage. Messy hot dogs pay but earn no tip.

Does wasting food cost money?

Yes: each bun or sausage you trash or drop off the cart costs $1, but your money never goes below $0.

Can I turn off the music?

Yes: pause the game and toggle Music or Sound.
