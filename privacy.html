Privacy Policy

Hot Dog Cart · Effective November 17, 2026

Summary

Hot Dog Cart does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or other personal information. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, best day and settings (music and sound) are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad between days, and an optional ad you can choose to watch to continue after a game over. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device information such as model, operating system version and screen size
  • The device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps, completions) and diagnostic data

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: on iOS 14.5 and later the game asks permission before ads can use your advertising identifier. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice from Privacy options in the game's pause menu.

Children

Hot Dog Cart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@toasterfireapps.com.
